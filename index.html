How to use
  1. Pick a scenario and press Load scenario.
  2. Move the date (Step 1). Crossing a 1st credits that month’s bonus.
  3. Add activity: Scan SKU, add points or litres, or PCC (Steps 2 and 3).
  4. Check the phone (Prastavana tab) and the Bonus ledger.
  5. For insurance, tap Activate Insurance in the app, submit the form, then press Provider confirms activation.

8. PRD v1.1 rules used
  • Classic: 60,000 points OR 5,000 L. Premium: 150,000 points OR 9,000 L. Whichever comes first.
  • Qualification points = scan base + scheme + approved PCC Coupon/SKU. Excluded: welcome bonus, Prastavana bonus, PCC Ad hoc.
  • Only bonus is monthly: Classic 4,000, Premium 10,000, credited on the 1st for the month just ended.
  • Credit = (rate × months from April) − already credited. This pays back earlier months and the Classic to Premium balance.
  • Qualifying on any date in a month counts that whole month. March is credited on 1 Apr 2027.
  • Blocked painters get 0. After reactivation, missed points are added through the API as ad hoc points.
  • Insurance becomes Active only after provider confirmation, never at handoff.
